1Rezepte redesign · library, detail, shopping

A warmer, denser Rezepte.

Dark mode kept, paprika kept. The brand evolves from paprika-on-black buttons to a paprika and honey palette with a real display serif (Newsreader) and a friendlier sans (Figtree). Everything is grounded in theme.css tokens from the repo.

Fixing the pains
  • Tag clutter: cards show one category; tags move to the filter rail on the left.
  • Empty space: content spans the full width with a filter rail, planner strip and a two-column shopping layout.
  • Frequently bought: 8 chips max, alphabetical, one "show all" — no per-chip ×.
  • Titles never truncate: editorial rows give titles a full line.
New
  • Week planner strip on the library, and a dedicated Plan destination.
  • Check-off with a "Bought today" section and bought history per list.
  • Recently cooked carousel; "Cooked" action on the detail page feeds it.
  • Shopping overview becomes a real home: lists, this week, loyalty cards side by side.
